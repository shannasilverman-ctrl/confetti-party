# Logo refresh opportunity

The current production lockup remains intentionally unchanged until the owner
approves a replacement. Logo exploration is not allowed to silently ride along
with a product release.

## Product brief

Confetti is a joyful cohost: it turns the mental load between “we should host”
and “I am at the party” into one calm plan. The identity must balance
celebration with control across birthdays, holidays, weddings, Shabbat, BBQs,
game day, and casual dinners.

## Required outcomes

- The name reads instantly as **Confetti**.
- Letter spacing remains optically correct; no decorated letter may detach
  from the word.
- The mark feels party-oriented without becoming a dating-app heart, generic
  sparkle, balloon cluster, invitation envelope, or party-popper emoji.
- The system works over white, deep plum, and real party photography.
- The icon remains recognizable at 16, 24, 32, 48, 72, and 104 pixels.
- Primary and reversed lockups meet contrast requirements.
- Motion, if used, is a short celebration response and respects
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Approval gate

Present a small final round in context: navigation at mobile and desktop,
app icon, favicon, event workspace, and the video-led landing hero. Compare the
options at actual size. Production files change only after explicit approval.
